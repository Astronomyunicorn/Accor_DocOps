# Style Guide

- **Language**: EN primary, FR mirror (mark `outdated: true` when lagging).
- **Tone**: Practical, concise, imperative where needed.
- **Headings**: `# H1` per page, `## H2` sections.
- **Front matter**: required for every page.
- **Screenshots/Diagrams**: prefer SVG/PNG; store under `docs/media`.
- **Code blocks**: fenced triple backticks with language.
- **ITIL names**: 
  - Incident Management
  - Problem Management
  - Change Enablement (Change Management)
  - Configuration Management (CMDB)
  - Service Catalog Management
